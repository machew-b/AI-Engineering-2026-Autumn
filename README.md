## AI Engineering 2026 Autumn

**AI Engineering 2026 Autumn**—officially *AI Engineering Practice Course*—is a practical course offered by the **Matsuo–Iwasawa Laboratory at the University of Tokyo**, through the Department of Technology Management for Innovation.

This course introduces students to the end-to-end process of building and improving real AI systems using modern engineering practices, tools, and evaluation methods.

### Course focus

The course is designed to teach current AI-development practices systematically, with an emphasis on building and operating real AI systems. It covers the development cycle from:

1. AI-assisted coding
2. Requirements definition
3. MVP development
4. Model and system evaluation
5. Deployment
6. Monitoring and improvement

The course is primarily aimed at students, with some participation from working professionals affiliated with the Metaverse School of Engineering.
